# Suri research and source register

Checked **9 October 2026**. This is a partial first-party comparison, not proof that Suri's proposed combination is unique. Features, regional availability, and APIs can change; recheck sources before implementation or public claims.

## Existing products

| Product | Verified scope | Suri implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Norton Genie and Scam Protection | User-submitted content checking; current Norton documentation also describes iPhone Safe SMS | Screenshot checking and SMS protection are established categories |
| Bitdefender Scamio and Scam Protection Pro | Submitted text/images/QRs; notification and Messenger/chat monitoring documented as Android-only | Do not claim cross-app notification screening is new or automatically available on iPhone |
| ScamShield | iOS on-device unknown-SMS filtering and manual checks; optimized for Singapore | Local filtering alone is not a novelty; Philippine effectiveness needs its own evaluation |
| Truecaller for iPhone | Official FAQ describes local SMS analysis | Privacy/offline alone is insufficient differentiation |

Scamio claims multilingual input but English replies. Do not infer that competitors cannot understand Taglish. Current Philippine subscription/App Store availability and the complete processing architecture of every vendor feature were not established by this comparison. Historical launch-country lists are not current availability evidence.

## Philippine evidence and references

- [BSP fraud/scam guide](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Media_and_Research/Primers%20Faqs/Protect_yourself_from_Fraud_and_Scam.pdf): examples of text scams, phishing, impersonation, and independent verification guidance. Use dated material and contextual wording; do not infer every urgent message is fraudulent.
- [BSP verifier](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/FinancialStability/BSPVerifier.aspx): financial-institution information and reporting categories. Absence from a directory is not by itself proof of a scam.
- [PSA messaging-app phishing advisory](https://psa.gov.ph/system/files/philsys/Public%20Advisory%20on%20Phishing%20Scams%20and%20Offers%20of%20Assistance%20in%20Downloading%20the%20Digital%20National%20ID%20via%20Messaging%20Apps.pdf): a documented Philippine messaging-app scenario, issued 13 February 2025.
- [Scam Watch Pilipinas FAQ](https://scamwatchpilipinas.com/faqs/): education/referrals, not proof of an automatic local classifier or a Suri partnership.
- [Government reporting article](https://www.pna.gov.ph/index.php/articles/1228318): eGov eReport and CICC reporting context. Reverify current channel details before embedding them.
- [NPC AI guidance register](https://privacy.gov.ph/pips-and-pics/advisories-circulars/): relevant privacy guidance for systems processing personal data. An implementation review is still needed.

## Differentiation hypotheses

Suri could distinguish its experience through low-friction capture, an understandable local explanation of requested actions, evidence-linked uncertainty, evaluated Philippine language/scenario quality, and consensual trusted-family assistance. These are proposed qualities, not verified competitive absences or measured superiority.

The defensible demo is a useful new local assessment with cloud unavailable, followed by a separately sourced online investigation or a genuinely delivered opted-in family alert.

## Research limits

Sources establish APIs, published features, and examples. They do not establish Suri's accuracy, older-user comprehension, background runtime, real-world prevention outcomes, or branding availability. Resolve those through actual device tests and appropriately scoped user evaluation.
